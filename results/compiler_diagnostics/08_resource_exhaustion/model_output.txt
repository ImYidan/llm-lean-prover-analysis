### Abstract Step-by-Step Plan

1. **Prove that `2016 ∈ S`:**
   - Choose `m = 1837 = 11 × 167`.
   - Verify that `m` has exactly four divisors: `1, 11, 167, 1837`.
   - Verify that their sum is `1 + 11 + 167 + 1837 = 2016`.

2. **Prove that for every other `n ∈ {2010, ..., 2019}`, `n ∉ S`:**
   - **Substep 2.1:** Check that no cube of a prime `p` has sum of divisors in `[2010, 2019]`.
     - Calculate sums for `p = 7, 11, 13` and observe they are either too small or too large.
