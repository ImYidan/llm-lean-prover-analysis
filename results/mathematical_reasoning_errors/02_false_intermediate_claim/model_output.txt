### Detailed Proof and Analysis

#### Understanding the Problem

We need to evaluate the sum:
$$
S = \sum_{k=1}^{p-2} k^{-1} (k+1)^{-1} \pmod{p}
$$
where $ p \geq 7 $ is a prime number and all operations are in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$.

#### Key Observations and Simplifications

1. **Field Structure**: Since $p$ is prime, $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ is a field, and every non-zero element has a multiplicative inverse. In particular, for $1 \leq k \leq p-2$, both $k$ and $k+1$ are non-zero modulo $p$.

2. **Telescoping Approach**: The term $k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$ can be rewritten as $(k+1 - k)k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1}$. However, this doesn't immediately give a telescoping sum because of the coefficient $(k+1 - k) = 1$. Let's check this carefully:
   $$
   (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = (k+1 - k)(k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}) = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}.
   $$
   So, indeed:
   $$
   k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1}.
   $$

3. **Telescoping Sum**: Using the above identity, the sum $S$ becomes:
   $$
   S = \sum_{k=1}^{p-2} \left( (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} \right) = (p-1)^{-1} - 1^{-1}.
   $$
   But $(p-1) \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$, so $(p-1)^{-1} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$. Therefore:
   $$
   S \equiv -1 - 1 = -2 \equiv p - 2 \pmod{p}.
   $$
   **Wait a minute!** This doesn't match the expected result of $2$. Did I make a mistake?

   **Yes!** Actually, the sum is from $k = 1$ to $k = p-2$, so the telescoping gives:
   $$
   S = (2^{-1} - 1^{-1}) + (3^{-1} - 2^{-1}) + \cdots + ((p-1)^{-1} - (p-2)^{-1}) = (p-1)^{-1} - 1^{-1}.
   $$
   So the calculation was correct. But the expected answer in the problem is $2$, whereas I got $-2 \equiv p - 2$. Are these equal modulo $p$? Only if $p - 2 \equiv 2 \pmod{p}$, i.e., $p \mid 4$. But $p \geq 7$, so this is not the case unless $p = 2$, which it isn't. 

   **Hmm, perhaps there is a misunderstanding of the problem statement in Lean.**

#### Checking Lean's Statement

The Lean theorem states:
```lean4
(∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹) = 2
```

But notice that `(k : ZMod p) + 1` is `(k + 1 : ZMod p)`, whereas in my calculation, I assumed it was `(k + 1)^{-1}` in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$. 

But in Lean, `(k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹` is indeed $k^{-1} (k+1)^{-1}$, as expected. 

**But perhaps the issue is with the indices?**

In Lean, `Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2)` is the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, p-2\}$, as expected. 

But perhaps I made a mistake in assuming that $(p-1)^{-1} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$? No, because $p-1 \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$, and $(-1)^{-1} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$. 

But in Lean, when $k = p-2$, the term is $(p-2)^{-1} \cdot (p-1)^{-1}$. So the last term is correct. 

Alternatively, perhaps the problem expects $2$ modulo $p$, but Lean is comparing it to the integer $2$ in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$. 

But for $p = 7$, let's compute manually:
$$
S = 1^{-1} \cdot 2^{-1} + 2^{-1} \cdot 3^{-1} + \cdots + 5^{-1} \cdot 6^{-1}.
$$
Modulo 7:
$$
1^{-1} = 1, \quad 2^{-1} = 4, \quad 3^{-1} = 5, \quad 4^{-1} = 2, \quad 5^{-1} = 3, \quad 6^{-1} = 6.
$$
Thus:
$$
S \equiv 1 \cdot 4 + 4 \cdot 5 + 5 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 6 \equiv 4 + 20 + 10 + 6 + 18 \equiv 4 + 6 + 3 + 6 + 4 \equiv 23 \equiv 2 \pmod{7}.
$$
Indeed, it gives $2$! 

But according to my telescoping, it should be $(p-1)^{-1} - 1^{-1} \equiv 6^{-1} - 1 \equiv 6 - 1 = 5 \equiv -2 \pmod{7}$. But $2 \neq 5 \pmod{7}$. 

**Where is the discrepancy?**

Ah! My telescoping was wrong. Actually:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} ( (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} ) = (p-1)^{-1} - 1^{-1},
$$
but the sum is $\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$, and $k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} \neq (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1}$. 

Let me re-examine that step:
$$
(k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = (k+1 - k)/(k(k+1)) = 1/(k(k+1)) = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}.
$$
No, this is correct in a field! Because $(k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = \frac{1}{k+1} - \frac{1}{k} = \frac{k - (k+1)}{k(k+1)} = \frac{-1}{k(k+1)} = -k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}.$$

**Ah! The sign was wrong!** 

Actually:
$$
(k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = -k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}.
$$
Thus:
$$
k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = -( (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} ) = k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}.
$$

Then the sum becomes:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} (k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}) = 1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1}.
$$

Now, $(p-1)^{-1} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$, so:
$$
S \equiv 1 - (-1) = 2 \pmod{p}.
$$

This matches the manual calculation for $p=7$. 

**Conclusion**: The correct identity is $k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}$ (in a field of characteristic not 2), and the telescoping gives $1 - (p-1)^{-1} \equiv 2 \pmod{p}$.

But wait, let me rederive the identity carefully:
$$
k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1} = \frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k+1} = \frac{(k+1) - k}{k(k+1)} = \frac{1}{k(k+1)} = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}.
$$
No, this again gives $k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$. 

But earlier, I had $(k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = -k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$. 

But now it seems $k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$. 

Which is correct? 

Let's take $k = 1$ in $\mathbb{R}$:
$$
1 - 1/2 = 1/2 = 1 \cdot (1/2).
$$
Yes, correct. 

But previously, I thought $(k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = -k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$. For $k=1$:
$$
1/2 - 1 = -1/2 = -1 \cdot (1/2).
$$
Also correct. 

Thus, both identities are correct:
$$
k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}, \quad (k+1)^{-1} - k^{-1} = -k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}.
$$

But then, if we write $k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}$, the sum becomes:
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} (k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}) = 1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1}.
$$
But for $p=7$, $1^{-1} - 6^{-1} \equiv 1 - 6 \equiv -5 \equiv 2 \pmod{7}$, which matches. 

But earlier, I thought it was $(p-1)^{-1} - 1^{-1}$. The confusion arose because I had mixed up the order of terms. 

**The correct telescoping is indeed:**
$$
\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} (k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}) = 1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1}.
$$

But from the first identity, $k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}$, so the sum is also $\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = 1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1} \equiv 2 \pmod{p}$.

**Thus, everything is consistent.**

#### Formal Proof Sketch

1. **Field Properties**: For each $k \in \{1, \ldots, p-2\}$, both $k$ and $k+1$ are invertible modulo $p$.
2. **Key Identity**: For any invertible $k$, $k^{-1} \cdot (k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}$ in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$. 
   - Proof: $k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1} = \frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k+1} = \frac{1}{k(k+1)} = k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1}$.
   - But note that in Lean, we're working in a field, so the calculation is valid.
3. **Telescoping Sum**: $\sum_{k=1}^{p-2} (k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}) = 1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1}$.
4. **Simplify $(p-1)^{-1}$**: Since $p-1 \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$, $(p-1)^{-1} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$.
5. **Final Result**: $1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1} \equiv 1 - (-1) = 2 \pmod{p}$.

#### Step-by-Step Abstract Plan

1. **Verify Invertibility**: For $1 \leq k \leq p-2$, both $k$ and $k+1$ are non-zero modulo $p$, hence invertible.
2. **Prove the Identity**: For each $k$, show that $k^{-1}(k+1)^{-1} = k^{-1} - (k+1)^{-1}$ in $\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$.
   - This can be done by direct computation in the field.
3. **Telescoping Sum**: Substitute the identity into the sum and telescope to get $1^{-1} - (p-1)^{-1}$.
4. **Simplify $(p-1)^{-1}$**: Compute $(p-1)^{-1} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$.
5. **Final Simplification**: Combine to get $1 - (-1) = 2$.

### Complete Lean 4 Proof

```lean4
theorem mathd_numbertheory_764 (p : ℕ) (h₀ : Nat.Prime p) (h₁ : 7 ≤ p) :
    (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹) = 2 := by
  have h_main : (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹) = 2 := by
    haveI := Fact.mk h₀
    have h₂ : p ≥ 7 := by exact_mod_cast h₁
    have h₃ : p ≥ 2 := by linarith
    have h₄ : (p : ℕ) - 2 + 1 ≥ 6 := by
      have h₅ : p ≥ 7 := by exact_mod_cast h₁
      omega
    -- Step 1: Prove the key identity in the field ZMod p
    have h₅ : ∀ (k : ℕ), 1 ≤ k → k ≤ p - 2 → (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ = (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ := by
      intro k hk₁ hk₂
      have h₆ : (k : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := by
        have h₇ : (k : ℕ) < p := by
          have h₈ : k ≤ p - 2 := hk₂
          have h₉ : p - 2 < p := by
            have h₁₀ : p ≥ 2 := by linarith
            omega
          omega
        have h₈ : (k : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := by
          intro h₉
          have h₁₀ : (k : ZMod p) = 0 := h₉
          have h₁₁ : (p : ℕ) ∣ k := by
            simpa [h₁₀] using ZMod.nat_cast_zmod_eq_zero_iff_dvd k p
          have h₁₂ : p ≤ k := Nat.le_of_dvd (by omega) h₁₁
          omega
        exact h₈
      have h₇ : ((k : ZMod p) + 1 : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := by
        have h₈ : (k : ℕ) < p - 1 := by
          have h₉ : k ≤ p - 2 := hk₂
          have h₁₀ : p - 2 < p - 1 := by
            have h₁₁ : p ≥ 2 := by linarith
            omega
          omega
        have h₉ : (k : ℕ) + 1 < p := by
          omega
        have h₁₀ : ((k : ZMod p) + 1 : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := by
          intro h₁₁
          have h₁₂ : ((k : ZMod p) + 1 : ZMod p) = 0 := h₁₁
          have h₁₃ : (p : ℕ) ∣ (k + 1) := by
            simpa [h₁₂] using ZMod.nat_cast_zmod_eq_zero_iff_dvd (k + 1) p
          have h₁₄ : p ≤ k + 1 := Nat.le_of_dvd (by omega) h₁₃
          omega
        exact h₁₀
      -- Use the identity (k⁻¹ - (k+1)⁻¹) = k⁻¹ * (k+1)⁻¹ in the field
      have h₈ : (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ = (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ := by
        have h₉ : (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ = ((k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹) := by
          calc
            (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ = (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹ := rfl
            _ = ((k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹) := by
              have h₁₀ : (k : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := h₆
              have h₁₁ : ((k : ZMod p) + 1 : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := h₇
              field_simp [h₁₀, h₁₁]
              <;> ring_nf
              <;> field_simp [h₁₀, h₁₁]
              <;> ring_nf
              <;> simp_all [ZMod.nat_cast_self]
              <;> norm_num
              <;>
              (try omega) <;>
              (try
                {
                  simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]
                  <;> omega
                })
        rw [h₉]
      rw [h₈]
      <;>
      (try omega) <;>
      (try
        {
          simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]
          <;> omega
        })
    -- Step 2: Rewrite the sum using the key identity
    have h₆ : (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), (k : ZMod p)⁻¹ * ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹) = (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), ((k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹)) := by
      apply Finset.sum_congr rfl
      intro k hk
      have h₇ : 1 ≤ k := by
        simp [Finset.mem_Icc] at hk
        linarith
      have h₈ : k ≤ p - 2 := by
        simp [Finset.mem_Icc] at hk
        linarith
      rw [h₅ k h₇ h₈]
    rw [h₆]
    -- Step 3: Telescope the sum
    have h₇ : (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), ((k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹)) = (1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((p - 2 : ℕ) : ZMod p + 1)⁻¹ := by
      have h₈ : ∀ (n : ℕ), (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 n, ((k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹)) = (1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((n : ℕ) : ZMod p + 1)⁻¹ := by
        intro n
        induction n with
        | zero =>
          simp
        | succ n ih =>
          rw [Finset.sum_Icc_succ_top (by omega : 1 ≤ n.succ), ih]
          <;> simp [Nat.cast_add, Nat.cast_one, add_assoc]
          <;>
          (try ring_nf) <;>
          (try norm_num) <;>
          (try simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]) <;>
          (try omega)
          <;>
          (try
            {
              field_simp [show (p : ℕ) ≠ 0 by linarith]
              <;> ring_nf
              <;> simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]
              <;> omega
            })
          <;>
          (try
            {
              simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]
              <;> omega
            })
      have h₉ : (∑ k in Finset.Icc 1 (p - 2), ((k : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((k : ZMod p) + 1)⁻¹)) = (1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ - ((p - 2 : ℕ) : ZMod p + 1)⁻¹ := by
        have h₁₀ := h₈ (p - 2)
        simpa using h₁₀
      rw [h₉]
    rw [h₇]
    -- Step 4: Simplify (p - 2 : ℕ) : ZMod p + 1 to (p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p
    have h₈ : ((p - 2 : ℕ) : ZMod p) + 1 = (p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p := by
      have h₉ : (p : ℕ) ≥ 7 := by exact_mod_cast h₁
      have h₁₀ : (p : ℕ) ≥ 2 := by linarith
      have h₁₁ : (p - 2 : ℕ) + 1 = p - 1 := by
        have h₁₂ : p ≥ 2 := by linarith
        have h₁₃ : p - 2 + 1 = p - 1 := by
          omega
        exact h₁₃
      have h₁₂ : ((p - 2 : ℕ) : ZMod p) + 1 = (p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p := by
        norm_cast at h₁₁ ⊢
        <;>
        (try simp_all [ZMod.nat_cast_self]) <;>
        (try omega) <;>
        (try
          {
            simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]
            <;> omega
          })
      exact h₁₂
    rw [h₈]
    -- Step 5: Simplify (p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p to -1
    have h₉ : ((p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p) = -1 := by
      have h₁₀ : (p : ℕ) ≥ 7 := by exact_mod_cast h₁
      have h₁₁ : (p : ℕ) ≥ 2 := by linarith
      have h₁₂ : ((p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p) = -1 := by
        have h₁₃ : (p : ℕ) ≠ 0 := by linarith
        have h₁₄ : ((p - 1 : ℕ) : ZMod p) = -1 := by
          norm_num [ZMod.nat_cast_self]
          <;>
          (try simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]) <;>
          (try omega)
          <;>
          (try
            {
              cases p with
              | zero => contradiction
              | succ p' =>
                cases p' with
                | zero => contradiction
                | succ p'' =>
                  simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast, Nat.mod_eq_of_lt]
                  <;> ring_nf at *
                  <;> omega
            })
        exact h₁₄
      exact h₁₂
    rw [h₉]
    -- Step 6: Simplify the expression to 2
    have h₁₀ : (1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ - (-1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ = 2 := by
      have h₁₁ : (1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ = (1 : ZMod p) := by
        simp
      rw [h₁₁]
      have h₁₂ : (-1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ = -1 := by
        have h₁₃ : (-1 : ZMod p) ≠ 0 := by
          intro h₁₄
          have h₁₅ : (p : ℕ) ∣ 1 := by
            simpa [h₁₄] using ZMod.nat_cast_zmod_eq_zero_iff_dvd 1 p
          have h₁₆ : p ≤ 1 := Nat.le_of_dvd (by norm_num) h₁₅
          linarith
        have h₁₄ : (-1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ = -1 := by
          have h₁₅ : (-1 : ZMod p) * (-1 : ZMod p) = 1 := by
            norm_num [ZMod.nat_cast_self]
            <;>
            (try simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]) <;>
            (try omega)
          have h₁₆ : (-1 : ZMod p)⁻¹ = -1 := by
            apply inv_eq_of_mul_eq_one_right
            simpa using h₁₅
          exact h₁₆
        exact h₁₄
      rw [h₁₂]
      norm_num [ZMod.nat_cast_self]
      <;>
      (try simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast]) <;>
      (try omega)
      <;>
      (try
        {
          cases p with
          | zero => contradiction
          | succ p' =>
            cases p' with
            | zero => contradiction
            | succ p'' =>
              simp_all [Fin.ext_iff, ZMod.val_nat_cast, Nat.mod_eq_of_lt]
              <;> ring_nf at *
              <;> omega
        })
    rw [h₁₀]
  exact h_main
```
